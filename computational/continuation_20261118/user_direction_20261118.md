# User direction

Keep the current continuation running under its existing scientific criteria.
Do not lower thresholds during this run. After the continuation finishes, if
there are still no complete passers, discuss selecting from the completed design
pool under a relaxed, explicitly documented ranking/screening scheme.

This instruction does not prevent repairing execution or data-format errors.
Record such repairs and preserve previous artifacts. Internal computational
thresholds are not official challenge requirements, and their failure does not
prove absence of experimental binding.
